You may extract this image with --appimage-extract, replace compatible
shared libraries under squashfs-root/usr/lib and plugins under usr/plugins,
and run squashfs-root/AppRun. No signature or technical lock prevents this.
The MIT application license imposes no restriction on modification or
reverse engineering for debugging modifications to LGPL libraries.

The AppImage bundles libraries and must be replaced with a rebuilt release
to obtain their updates. It is not a sandbox. Native application plugins
remain disabled by default; Qt platform and image-format plugins are distinct.
